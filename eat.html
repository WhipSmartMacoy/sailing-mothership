Just make food.

Recipes for lovers of healthy, good food.

You'll find recipes for 30-minute meals, dishes with 10 ingredients or fewer, options for specific diets, and food for entertaining. Most of our recipes are Paleo and can be adapted for Keto, Whole30, AIP, GAPS, allergy-free or vegetarian diets.

About “Paleo”

Most of our recipes leave out gluten, grains, corn, dairy, eggs, nuts and seeds, soy and nightshades, and avoid processed foods, emulsifiers, refined oils and sugars, dried legumes and artificial sweeteners. Recipes marked “Traditional” don't necessarily follow a specific diet.
