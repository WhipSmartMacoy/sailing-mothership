Super Easy Delish Chocolate Chess Pie

This is our paleo-friendly version of the chocolate pie served at the Angus Barn. It's amazingly easy and may become a regular in your rotation!

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 3 oz semisweet or bittersweet chocolate, chopped (I use Enjoy Life Semi-Sweet Mini Chips)
  • 1 cup sugar (I use coconut sugar)
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 unbaked pie shell from the freezer section (I use Wholly gluten-free pie crust)
  • Whipped cream for topping (optional)

You'll need: a bowl, measuring cups and spoons, oven mitts.

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Gently melt the butter and chocolate together. I use a regular pan on low heat instead of a double boiler.
  3. In a separate bowl, mix the sugar, eggs and vanilla. A spoon works fine.
  4. Add the melted chocolate mixture and stir until fully combined.
  5. Pour into the pie shell and bake until set, about 30–35 minutes. Mine usually takes 40 minutes or more.
  6. Let cool, then top with whipped cream if you like.

Notes

Paleo swaps: coconut sugar, Enjoy Life chocolate chips and a gluten-free crust. Refrigerate leftovers.
