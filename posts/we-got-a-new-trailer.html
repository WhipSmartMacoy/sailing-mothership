We got a new trailer!

We have a new-to-us trailer! The old one wasn't broken, but the story starts a little earlier. Watch the video (it has more sailing) or read on.

After six months of searching, we found one of the last Hobie Mirage Tandem Islands around, only six hours away. The drive up was lovely: scenic views, interesting architecture, bridges and underwater tunnels. (Tunnels make me a little nervous!) It was a nice trip... at least on the way up.

The kayak was ready for us at the paddlesports shop, and we headed home hoping for a relaxing drive back to our dogs. That's not what happened.

The blown tire

A trailer tire blew out. We made it to a gas station and parked safely, but nothing nearby had a spare in the right size, and everything was closing.

We called our insurance company, which promised a tow truck and even sent a confirmation text. The truck never came. Businesses closed at 8pm, including the fast food place across the street. By 1am we were stranded, maybe alone, with a makeshift lean-to in the woods nearby. It was scary.

A different insurance rep finally arranged a real tow truck, which didn't arrive until morning. We got home about 15 hours after the tire blew, without the boat. It arrived safely a few hours later.

Why we upgraded

We loved our lightweight aluminum Trailex trailer because it was so easy to move around the driveway, but its small tires weren't right for the long trip to Florida we had planned.

We found an EZLoader trailer on Craigslist for about $1,400. Billy modified it with 2x6 boards and moved the Hobie cradles over.

The EZLoader is longer, wider and heavier, so we practiced parking and backing up in an empty mall parking lot.

We're very happy with the EZLoader. It rides smoother and is far less stressful. And a spare tire is on order!
