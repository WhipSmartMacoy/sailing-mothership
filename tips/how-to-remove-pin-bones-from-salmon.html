How to Remove Pin Bones from Salmon

Mind yourself with the elusive pin bones in salmon!

Materials

  • A thawed salmon fillet
  • Kitchen or pin bone tweezers, or needle-nose pliers (I used workshop pliers, cleaned and sanitized first!)

Steps

  1. Lay the thawed fillet skin side down.
  2. Gently run your fingertips up and down the flesh to find the ends of the pin bones.
  3. Grip each bone firmly and pull in the direction it points.
  4. Repeat until they're all out.

Some pin bones are hard to find and you may not notice them until you're eating, so stay alert!
