Enter the Salad OXO Spinner

One of my favorite multi-use kitchen items! It's a bowl, a strainer and colander, and a salad spinner all in one. It's dishwasher safe, made of BPA-free plastic and stainless steel, and sturdy, with a non-slip knob and a gripping base that keeps it in place on the counter.

To wash greens or herbs, put them in the colander, rinse under running water (swishing them over the bowl), drain, then spin out the rest of the water. The lid has a built-in brake and locks down for storage. I keep mine in the fridge to store greens.

It comes in two sizes, and I recommend getting both!
