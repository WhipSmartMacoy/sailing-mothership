Enter the Salad OXO Spinner

This is one of my favorite multi-use kitchen items!

Is it a bowl?

  • A strainer and colander?
  • Is it easy to clean up?
  • A salad spinner?

Why yes! Yes, it is all that and more!

And one of my favorite things about it? Not only is it dishwasher safe, it’s also BPA-free plastic and stainless steel. It is super sturdy and has a non-slip knob and a gripping base so it stays put on your counter.

Any type of greens or herbs will be cleaner and fresher in this sweet salad spinner.

Simply toss your greens or herbs into the colander and rinse under running water. I actually do this over the bowl itself and swish it around a bit.

Then drain the water, place the colander into the bowl, and give it a spin.

You will quickly see how much water was still hugging those delicate delicious greens. Simply drain and replace the lid. Oh, and did I mention the lid has a built-in brake and locks down for storage?

I keep mine in the fridge to store all of my greens.

There are even two different sizes for you to choose from. Or better yet! Don’t choose! Get both!
